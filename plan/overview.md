Yes. I think there is a real business here, but I would make one important change to your framing.

I would not build a “SOCMINT company” first.

I would build an Intelligence company that happens to have SOCMINT as its core data engine.

That distinction is extremely important.

Nigeria is becoming an environment where businesses, political actors, governments, investors and brands are surrounded by enormous amounts of fragmented public information. Nigeria had roughly 107 million internet users and tens of millions of identifiable social-media audiences in early 2025; TikTok alone reported 37.4 million adults in its advertising audience, while LinkedIn reported 11 million members. At the same time, Nigeria's digital advertising economy is expanding, with one 2026 estimate putting online advertising and digital media at about $1.2B in 2025 and projecting continued double-digit growth.

The opportunity isn't simply to collect all this information.

The opportunity is to turn the information into decisions that somebody will pay for.
